The clearer EkkoMD brand mark is saved as `ekkomd-mark.svg`, unchanged from the official `favIcon.svg` linked in the live homepage document. It uses a violet gradient rounded tile with a white central mark, so it remains visible against both light and dark card backgrounds. The official 192px `favicon.png` was also inspected and matches the same brand mark. The faint footer wordmark remains in the folder for reference.
